BAI 5: CHON SAI BIEN THE NAIVE BAYES
So sinh vien: 120; train: 90; test: 30
MultinomialNB : accuracy = 0.8000
GaussianNB    : accuracy = 0.9000

Chuong trinh khong bao loi vi ca hai dac trung deu khong am.
MultinomialNB chap nhan so thuc khong am, nhung gio on va diem
khong phai so dem cac loai su kien trong mo hinh multinomial.
No bien hai dai luong khac don vi thanh thanh phan dem, lam sai
y nghia xac suat; doi don vi gio sang phut cung co the doi ket qua.
GaussianNB mo ta mat do lien tuc bang trung binh va phuong sai
tung dac trung theo lop, phu hop hon ve loai du lieu.
Accuracy tren mot tap nho khong chung minh gia dinh phan phoi dung.
