BAI 4: BO LOC TU VIET VA MULTINOMIALNB
Tap hoc: 225; tap test: 75; tu dien: 40
Accuracy tu viet      = 0.9467
Accuracy MultinomialNB = 0.9467
So du doan khac nhau  = 0
Sai so diem log lon nhat = 1.421e-14

Giai thich: score(k) = log P(k) + sum count(tu)*log P(tu|k).
P(tu|k) = (dem(tu,k)+alpha)/(tong_tu(k)+alpha*V).
Laplace tranh log(0); cong log tranh tran duoi khi nhan nhieu so nho.
Hai bo loc cung tap chia, cach tach tu, tu dien, alpha va tien nghiem,
nen cho cung diem (sai so so thuc rat nho) va cung du doan.
Tu ngoai tu dien bi bo qua; tin khong co tu da biet chi dung prior.
Neu lech: kiem tra lower(), tu mot ky tu, so lan lap, mau so alpha*V,
va viec chi hoc tu dien tren tap train, khong hoc lai tren tap test.
